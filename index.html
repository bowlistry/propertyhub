Common Questions
Frequently Asked
Who can live at Bowlistry? ▾
Bowlistry is exclusively open to employees of verified top-tier companies — including MNCs, Big 4 firms, top Indian corporations, and leading startups. A valid company email or employee ID is required during onboarding.
What is included in the monthly rent? ▾
All plans include: fully furnished room, 3 meals/day, WiFi, housekeeping, gym access, electricity, water, and security. Premium and Elite plans include additional services like co-working, laundry, and personal concierge.
What is the minimum stay duration? ▾
Our minimum stay is 3 months, with monthly renewals thereafter. We also offer short-stay plans (2–4 weeks) for certain locations at a slightly higher rate.
Is there a security deposit? ▾
Yes, a refundable security deposit of 2 months' rent is collected at the time of move-in. It is returned in full within 7 business days of your move-out, subject to no damage.
